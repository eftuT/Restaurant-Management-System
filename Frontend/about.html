The Story of SEN'Q

The word SEN'Q is a collection of food put in a special container called AGELGIL.

It is mainly practiced in the rural areas of Ethiopia to deliver food for farmers specifically who work in a team for crop gathering. At lunchtime, their wives come with food inside Agelgil which is called SEN'Q and also with a traditional drink called Tela.

Not only for farmers, it is also used for a person who will travel long way by foot. When he gets tired, he will rest and eat his SEN'Q whose mother or wife made it.

"When you think of food, you think of Ethiopian food."
